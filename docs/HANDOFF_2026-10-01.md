    `latents_sha256`.

  - Add a mutation test per dependency. A failure must leave reports unchanged.

  output and require:
  - `complete`, integrity and the known-failure controls;
  - the two start cells;
  - the four endpoint cells, *including* their displacement and relative-floor gates
    (all or none).

  Add a sentinel test proving prediction latents are never loaded on failure.

  - Version the classifier with `SearchConfig.version` (1 keeps v1 reproducible).
  - In v2, the reported pose is the best of the *independent searches*. A nuisance
    candidate that is better and farther than `restart_tolerance` gives
    `optimizer_unresolved` (reason `nuisance_better_basin`).
  - An unfinished nuisance challenge (status `budget`, or `fitted` but not converged)
    gives `optimizer_unresolved` (reason `nuisance_incomplete`). Add fixtures for both.
- **F4, wording.** Append a correction note to `MEAS-001_ENGINEERING.md`: identifiability
  is not validated; near-truth fits are promising; searches fail to reach the known

  charge the ledger. Fix the runbook claim. Do not reset either cap.

**b. v2 engineering pass.** First record a new decision (**D037**) before running.
- **Search change.** Add an independent refinement per search: Nelder–Mead (scipy) from
  *that search's own* best. New fields `refine_evaluations` (about 400) and
  `refine_simplex` (2, 2, 2, 2, 0.05). Phase `refine` is appended to `PHASES`. The
  evaluation cap includes the refinement.
  - Diagnosis it targets: searches end about 5 units apart, because coordinate (compass)
    search stalls in diagonal agent–block valleys.
  - A pooled initialization must **not** be used as restart evidence.
- **Thresholds, now separate.**
  - Fit acceptance τ_fit: keep the D032 percentile rule on calibration real queries.
  - Ambiguity tolerance τ_amb: per query and label-free, the residual increase for
    1.28-unit block offsets (8 directions) around the reported pose.
  - The near-optimal set is the candidates within τ_amb, and its diameter must be
    ≤ 2.56. Develop both rules on phase A.

  and preserve v1. Develop 1–2 settings on phase A, then run the selected one **once** on
  phase B with the rules fixed. Stop if it fails.
  - Budget: the 60-min engineering sub-cap has about 39 min left after the correction.
  - Each target takes about 25 s with refinement, so 24 targets is about 10 min.
- **Outcome.** A freeze-ready or not-ready proposal only. Calibration, assessment and
